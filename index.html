Collective Manifesto

BUILT ON COLLABORATION, NOT CONFORMITY.

Pylon Collective was founded at Toronto Metropolitan University to eliminate the artificial boundaries between departments. Rather than renting out isolated crew members, we operate as a unified filmmaking incubator: our commercial client work directly fuels our passion short films, music videos, and physical art exhibitions.
